# Building the explainer by hand

Use this only when the user has requested an HTML report and Python can't run. Load the `atlassian-design` skill and follow its `references/hand-built.md`, including its smart link rules. Then build the same explainer the renderer would: the sections, their order and what each one holds are in the Build step in this skill's `SKILL.md`, and the layout is below.

## Layout

- Fixed layout, 880px max width, so the diagram has room, on a `--ds-surface-sunken` background, with a 16px gutter at phone width. 48px above the header, and 32px between the header and the first card and between cards.
- With sample data, the mockup note is an "Example data" lozenge in discovery colours in the page's top right corner, 10px from the top and right edges, with the 12px information icon in `--ds-text-discovery` 4px before the text. It's positioned absolutely, so it takes no space.
- The header: a neutral skill tag reading `/how-it-works`, 20px tall, then 24px below it the question at 24px on a 28px line in medium weight (`--ds-font-weight-medium`), then 8px below that the line saying what the page is about and when ("Invoice search in Ledger, as of Oct 27, 2026.") in `body.large` and `--ds-text-subtle`.
- Each section is a flat card (`--ds-surface` plus a `--ds-border` border, `radius.large`, 16px padding). Its title is 14px semibold on a 21px line in `--ds-text`, with no icon, 16px above the body.
- Card text is body-large (16px on 24px) in `--ds-text-subtle`, with paragraphs 16px apart. Small text, such as the notes under Recent changes, is 14px on a 20px line in `--ds-text-subtlest`, 16px under what's above it.
- Where it happens is a diagram drawn as SVG with `role="img"`, named by a `title` and described by a `desc` with a sentence for each node and link, in a focusable region named "Diagram":
  - Every node has 12px of padding and a 6px radius. Its title is 14px medium `--ds-text`, its sub 12px `--ds-text-subtlest`, each in at most two lines of even length, broken at a space, or after `.`, `-` or `/` when no space break fits, and cut with an ellipsis, with the full text in the node's `<title>`.
  - The frame is `--ds-surface-sunken` with a `--ds-border` border and an 8px radius. Its title is 14px semibold `--ds-text` with "Code we read, at [commit]" in 12px `--ds-text-subtlest` under it, and 12px of padding around its nodes, which are `--ds-surface-raised` with a `--ds-border` border, each with its file name in the 12px code font. The trigger is `--ds-surface` with a `--ds-border` border. An outside node has a dashed (4 3) `--ds-border-bold` border, with "Outside the code we can see" over it in 12px bold `--ds-text-subtle`. Edges are 1.5px `--ds-border-bold` curves with an 8px head, and a link's verb is 12px `--ds-text-subtle` just above its edge.
  - The nodes are in columns, left to right: the trigger, then the files, then the outside nodes. In the frame, each file is a column after the furthest file that links to it, and a file that only feeds another sits a column before it. Nodes in a column are 32px apart and ordered so edges cross as little as they can, and each sits level with the nodes it joins where it can. Columns are at least 48px apart, more where a verb needs it. A verb sits by the end of its edge that no other edge shares, or midway when both are shared. An edge that skips a column passes through it, and one that loops back runs under the nodes between. The diagram draws at 1:1 when the card is wide enough, scales down to 80% with it, then scrolls sideways inside the card, never the page.
- Step by step is a numbered list, the steps divided by a `--ds-border` line with 16px on each side. Each number is a 20px circle with a 1px `--ds-text-subtlest` border, the number in the 14px code font at medium weight in `--ds-text-subtle`, 12px from the step and 2px down, so it's centred on the first line. Screen readers hear "Step 1." before the sentence. The step's parts are 8px apart: the sentence in body-large, the chips in a row that wraps with 4px gaps, then the disclosure.
- A code chip is the smart link chip with the 16px `angle-brackets` icon, centred in a 20px box, in `--ds-icon-subtle`, then the file name ("config.ts"), with the full path, the lines and the commit in its `title`, and the lines in visually hidden text after the name. A test chip has the test file's name, with the test's name added to its `title` and its hidden text.
- "Show the code" is a native `<details>` disclosure, closed at first: the `chevron-right` icon at 12px, as ADS sizes a control's chevron, 4px before the words, in 14px medium `--ds-text-subtle`, turning down when open, with no turn when `prefers-reduced-motion` is set. Inside, 8px below, the lines are after ADS `CodeBlock`: `--ds-background-neutral`, `radius.large`, the code font at 0.875em of 14px on a 20px line, 8px above and below. Line numbers are right-aligned in `--ds-text-subtlest`, 8px either side of them, in a column with a second `--ds-background-neutral` layer, and a gap between runs is a row holding "…". The box is a focusable region named "Code excerpt" that scrolls sideways inside itself, never the page.
- What can change this is a table after ADS `DynamicTable`, full width in 14px `--ds-text`: the header row in `body.small` bold `--ds-text-subtle` over a 2px `--ds-border` line, cells padded 8px all round but not at the table's edges, and set at the top, and a 2px `--ds-border` line under the last row. A cell's second line, the setting's kind or the steps it touches, is `body.small` `--ds-text-subtlest`, 2px below. A setting's name breaks only at its hyphens. Rows take `--ds-background-neutral-subtle-hovered` on hover. At 600px and narrower the header row is hidden and each row is a block, with each cell under its header's name in `body.small` bold and the rows divided by a `--ds-border` line with 8px on each side.
- What to check on the account is the `checkNote` line, then 16px below it the checkboxes, 8px apart, after ADS `Checkbox`: a 24px icon holding a 13px box with a 1.5px radius and a 1px `--ds-border-input` border on `--ds-background-input` (`--ds-background-input-hovered` on hover), filled `--ds-background-selected-bold` with an `--ds-icon-inverse` tick when checked, and a 2px `--ds-border-focused` outline inset 2px on keyboard focus. The native checkbox sits invisible over the icon, inside the label. The label is body-large `--ds-text-subtle`, 4px from the icon.
- What the docs and chat say has a row per source, divided like label rows: the smart link on its own line, then the claim in body-large, then the verdict lozenge and the meta in 14px `--ds-text-subtlest`, 8px apart. Then the count of questions in small text, then 16px below it a row per open bug: "Open bug", with its smart link 8px below. With no sources, the card says "Nothing in the docs or chat about this."
- Recent changes and Who owns it are label rows: they sit 32px under the card title, with the label in a 150px column in body-large `--ds-text-subtlest`, 12px from the content, and stack on phones. Rows are divided by a `--ds-border` line with 16px on each side. A row's line is followed 8px below by its smart links, in a row that wraps with 4px gaps, and where it came from sits 8px below that in small text. An owner with a link has only the link, and one with no owner recorded has the sentence.
- Suggested reply is the "Reply on" line in body-large `--ds-text-subtle` with the ticket's smart link, when the anchor was a ticket, then 16px below it the "Proposed. Check it before you send it." line in small text, then 16px below that the reply in body-large `--ds-text`, 12px inside a 2px `--ds-border` line on its left, then 12px below that a Copy button after ADS `Button` in its default appearance: 32px, 6px 12px of padding, `radius.medium`, 14px medium `--ds-text-subtle` on `--ds-background-neutral-subtle` with a 1px `--ds-border` border drawn over it, the 16px `copy` icon 6px from the word, and `--ds-background-neutral-subtle-hovered` and `-pressed` on hover and press. It says "Copied" for 1.5 seconds once it has copied. If the clipboard is blocked it selects the reply's text instead, so it can be copied by hand, and says "Text selected".
- The sources footer, 40px under the last card: a tag for each app searched, 4px apart, then 12px below them the lines as a bulleted list in 14px `--ds-text-subtle` text on a 21px line, 4px apart. A tag is ADS `Tag` in gray: 20px, a 1px `--ds-border-accent-gray-subtle` border inside 2px 3px of padding, `radius.small`, `body.small` in `--ds-text`, at most 180px with an ellipsis. A tag for an app that found nothing looks the same, its text saying so.
- Smart links follow `atlassian-design`'s rules, with these differences, from meeting prep's frames 6:2977 and 26:915:
  - The chip is a 28px inline flex row, its items centred 8px apart, with 3px of padding inside a 1px `--ds-border` border, or 7px on the right when it ends in text rather than a lozenge, `radius.medium` and `--ds-surface-raised`. Its border turns `--ds-border-accent-blue` on hover, and its background `--ds-background-selected` when pressed. On keyboard focus it takes the page's standard focus ring, a 2px `--ds-border-focused` outline 2px outside it, not the selected ring `atlassian-design`'s guide gives.
  - The 20px logo comes first, then the title in 14px regular `--ds-link` text on a 16px line with -0.1px tracking. The logo is from the `atlassian-design` skill's `render_kit_next/logos/`, for the app the link names, or else the Atlassian app its object comes from (Confluence, Jira, Goals, Projects or Loom). A third-party mark sits on a white tile with a 1px `--ds-border` border at `radius.tile`, white in dark mode too. A link whose app has no logo takes its ADS object icon at 16px, centred in a 20px box, in its accent colour, and any other icon in `--ds-icon-subtle`.
  - A status is a 20px lozenge after the title: a 1px border in its tone's subtle border colour, drawn inside 2px 4px of padding, with `body.small` at regular weight.
  - The app's name isn't shown. It goes in visually hidden text after the title.
- Support light and dark mode, with no horizontal scroll at 375px.
